about

What it is

A guided workshop that first trains safe blindfold dancing, then moves into a long silent block where the dancing becomes slow, minimal and focused. The music and the vocabulary are Brazilian Zouk.

Who it’s for

  • Brazilian Zouk dancers from improver level up. Below intermediate it may be hard.
  • The group is Zouk dancers. We may accept one or two advanced leaders or followers from other partner dances, as a rare exception, if we know them to be safe, musical and attentive to their partner.
  • Solo registration is welcome. Rotation is part of the format.
  • 18+ only.

format

Part 1 — Preparation (≈1.5h)

  • Warm-up, grounding, sensory orientation
  • Close-embrace safety and boundaries, shown and practiced
  • Dancing without sight: collisions, touch, exploration
  • Getting used to the blindfold, step by step

Part 2 — Silent Block (≈1.5h)

  • Blindfolded, no talking. Safety exceptions apply.
  • Rotation, with moments of anonymity
  • New senses, new connections
  • Debrief and grounding to close

Rotation & pairing

  • Rotation runs through both parts. Solo sign-ups are the norm.

  • We suggest using this only when you must. Meeting everyone fresh is part of the experience.

safety & consent

Consent baseline
  • No intimate touch, no surprises, no testing boundaries. Everything stays within normal close-embrace partner-dance limits.
  • No romantic escalation. No kissing, sexual comments, whispering, or “going beyond dancing”.
  • No-go zones are listed, shown and repeated before the silent block.
Yellow / Red system (practiced)
  • Yellow: reduce intensity / slow down / increase space.
  • Red: full stop. Freeze and give space immediately.
  • No explanation needed. Team members watch and can step in.
Touch boundaries (what’s allowed)

Close embrace is part of the format. Within limits that we show you, the workshop may include:

  • Body waves (frame-based and open-position waves)
  • Chest-to-chest contact and kizomba-style hip contact within demonstrated boundaries

Not allowed: no-go zones, breathing in ears, kissing, or hands anywhere outside the contact points we taught.

Movement rules (blindfold part)
  • No lifts. No dips. No broad movements.
  • Head movement is minimal and strictly guided. No fast or large-range head motions.
  • Travelling only when the exercise allows it. The team can stop risky movement at once.
The Silent Block rule (blindfolds)

Blindfolds stay on in the dance space for the whole silent block.

Need water, the restroom or support? Raise your hand. A team member walks you to a private safe area, away from the dance floor. There you may take the blindfold off.

You can usually come back. We may refuse if safety or the group needs it: medical concern, repeated distress, not following instructions, disruptive behaviour, or risk to yourself or others.

If you cannot come back, you can rest in the chill area and may join the final debrief. No refund in this case.

Signals (silent block)
  • Stop with partner: trained hand squeeze → freeze + give space.
  • Call a team member: raise your hand. The team is trained to notice fast.
  • Audible exception: if you must, a short call for help is allowed. Safety beats silence.
Hygiene & sobriety
  • Please come sober. No alcohol or recreational drugs before or during the workshop.
  • No perfume. Odourless antiperspirant is fine. Shower before you come.
  • Bring a fresh shirt.
  • No rings, jewellery or watches. Short nails.

Open the full Q&A
When and where is it?
Monday 25 May 2026, in the Amsterdam area close to the center. Time window is approximately 17:00–20:00, plus a closing debrief and grounding. The venue is already booked; the exact address will be shared with confirmed participants only, no later than 20 May 2026.
Why is the venue shared only with confirmed participants?
For participant safety and privacy. The confirmed venue is shared only with registered participants.
How long is the workshop and how is it structured?
About 3 hours total, split into two ~1.5-hour sections:
  1. Preparation & training (signals, boundaries, safe blindfold dancing)
  2. The ~1.5-hour Silent Blindfold Block (no participant talking; safety exceptions exist), followed by a debrief/grounding.
Is there a break?
Yes, one main break between the two sections. The first part is also more flexible for quick step-outs if needed.
Who is this workshop for?
Brazilian Zouk dancers from improver level up. The dance and the music are Zouk, and below intermediate it may be hard. We may accept one or two advanced dancers from other partner dances as a rare exception, if we know them to be safe, musical and attentive.
Can I join if I’ve never danced blindfolded?
Yes. The first part includes gradual accommodation and safety training. If you have a known strong fear of darkness or blindfolding, this workshop may not be suitable.
Is it okay if I’m anxious about blindfolding?
Many people are. Clear rules and a trained team are there for you. If anxiety comes up during the workshop, the team helps.
Is it okay if I know in advance I can’t stay blindfolded for the full silent block?
If you already know you won’t be able to stay in the Silent Block for the full duration, it’s recommended not to join.
Is registration open?
Yes. The form is public. The workshop is for Zouk dancers and we confirm applications by hand.
What is the registration flow?
Apply, verify your email, wait for our review. If accepted, you get a confirmation email with a payment link. We review for Zouk fit, safety and role balance.
Do I need to bring ID?
Yes. Bring a government ID (passport or driving licence) with the name you registered. We may check it at the door.
Can organizers reject an application?
Yes. We aim to be open and inclusive, but we may decline any application, for safety, balance or any other reason, with or without explanation.
Is there an age limit?
Yes, 18+ only.
Can I register solo?
Yes. Solo registration is welcome.
Can I register with a partner?
Yes. Add a note about your partner in the registration form; we will try to add you both if spots allow, or tell you otherwise.
Will there be partner rotation?
Yes, rotation is frequent and part of the format, including moments where you may not know who you’re dancing with.
Can I opt out of rotation?
No. Rotation is integral to the workshop design.
Is there any way to avoid dancing with a specific person?
Yes. Tell us confidentially who you do not want to dance with. No questions asked, rotations adjusted. We still suggest meeting everyone fresh if you can.
When should I submit a “do not pair” request?
Preferably as soon as the participant list is published, but it can also be submitted at the venue.
Will my “do not pair” request be confidential?
Yes. It is handled confidentially.
What if multiple “do not pair” requests create rotation issues?
Rotations will be adjusted to satisfy the constraints.
Are roles fixed (Leader/Follower)?
Yes. The workshop uses fixed Leader/Follower roles for structure and safety, with strict parity.
Can I switch roles?
Role changes are limited. Some exercises may include brief role swaps when the exercise dictates it, and participants are encouraged to follow the exercise for safety reasons. Exceptions may be allowed case-by-case for highly experienced dancers.
What’s the baseline consent rule?
No intimate touch, no surprises, no testing boundaries. Everything stays within socially appropriate close-embrace partner-dance boundaries.
Are there explicit no-go zones?
Yes. No-go zones will be clearly listed, shown, practiced, and repeated before the Silent Block.
Is romantic escalation allowed?
No. Romantic/sexual escalation is explicitly prohibited.
Is whispering or talking allowed during the Silent Block?
No participant talking during the Silent Block, with safety exceptions only.
Are sensual movements allowed?
Some sensual movements are permitted within defined limits and boundaries shown in the first part. Consent and no-go-zone rules always apply.
Is chest-to-chest contact allowed?
Yes, within defined limits demonstrated and reinforced in training.
What are the safety signals during the Silent Block?
They will be trained in the first section:
  • Partner stop: trained hand squeeze → freeze + give space
  • Call a team member: raise your hand
  • Audible exception: allowed only if absolutely necessary (safety overrides silence)
What is the “Silent Blindfold Block” blindfold rule?
Blindfolds stay on in the dance space for the whole silent block.
What if I need water, restroom, or support during the Silent Block?
Raise your hand. A team member will escort you to a private safe area (separate from the dance space). Water is typically brought to you; restroom support depends on venue setup.
Can I remove my blindfold in the safe area?
Yes, if necessary. The goal is to support you in the safest way possible.
Can I return to the Silent Block after removing the blindfold in the safe area?
Usually yes, but the team may decline return for safety or integrity reasons (e.g., medical concern, repeated distress, not following instructions, disruptive behavior, or risk to self/others).
If I’m not allowed to return to the Silent Block, what happens?
You can rest in a chill area and may be allowed to join the debrief.
Is there a refund if I can’t return to the Silent Block?
No.
What movements are prohibited?
No lifts, no dips, no broad movements, no broad head movements. Head movement is minimal and strictly guided, no fast or large-range head motion.
Is traveling allowed?
In some exercises, yes, when safety allows. We teach it in part 1, and the team may stop unsafe movement.
Do height/strength differences matter?
The safety rule applies to everyone regardless of size or strength: you must dance safely and respect boundaries. Inside the safety limits, no differences should matter.
Will the music be very loud?
Volume will be curated for comfort; ear protection should not be necessary.
Should I come sober?
Yes. A clear “please come sober” policy applies.
What should I wear / bring?
Comfortable dance clothes; nothing restrictive or complicated. Bring a water bottle and (recommended) a fresh shirt for the break. Warm layers may be advised depending on venue temperature.
What should I avoid wearing/bringing?
No jewelry, rings, watches, sharp objects or sharp clothing elements. Short nails are strongly recommended.
Are blindfolds provided?
Yes. Blindfolds are included: new, adjustable, no pressure on the eyes, tested for long wear, and yours to keep.
What about glasses or contact lenses?
Glasses should be removed for the blindfolded part. Contact lenses are a personal choice; consult organizers if unsure.
Can I store valuables safely?
Small valuables may be left with the team members for safekeeping, but participants are advised to bring as little as possible.
Are photos/videos taken?
Photos/videos may be taken in the first part and during the debrief. The blindfolded Silent Block is strictly no recording.
Can I opt out of being recorded?
Yes, opt-out is available. You can notify the organizer in advance or at check-in.

Will the workshop be recorded during the silent blindfolded part?
No. Never.
What language is the workshop taught in?
English.

16 couples / 32 people total. Confirmation depends on safety review and role balance.

Water will be available.

What if I have special accessibility needs?
This is a specialised format, so please judge for yourself whether it suits you. If you have specific needs or doubts, contact the organizer in advance.
